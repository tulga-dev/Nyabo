# The June 2026 tax package — what was adopted, when, and what was not

*Generated from the seed on 2026-09-08 by the legal-citation pass; the quotes are verbatim from the saved copies of the texts listed in docs/legal/README.md. Regenerate rather than edit the tables by hand.*

Four laws of **26 June 2026** amend the VAT Law, the CIT Law, the PIT Law and the General
Taxation Law. Their standalone texts were not located on legalinfo (search pages are
JavaScript-rendered); every fact below comes from the amendment notes printed in the
consolidated laws, cross-checked with ikon.mn's adoption article of 26 June 2026
(https://ikon.mn/n/3ojp: «… Татварын багц хуулийн төслийг эцэслэн батлалаа. Оролцсон
гишүүдийн 87.1 хувийн саналаар дөрвөн хуулийн төслийг баталсан бөгөөд 2027 оны нэгдүгээр
сарын 1-нээс хэрэгжиж эхэлнэ.»).
